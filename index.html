Import locations
KML, GPX, TCX, FIT, GeoJSON, CSV, a whole ZIP, Strava account export, or a JSON export from Google Timeline or Snapchat.

You can download Strava's archive from Settings → My Account → Download your account. You can also download Google Timeline from Google Maps on mobile, or Snapchat from accounts.snapchat.com → My Data → Export JSON Files + Ranking And Location. Note not all footsteps data is sent by Snapchat.

Strava
Syncing all your activities from Strava.

Requires a subscription and needs generating credentials.

  1. Open strava.com/settings/api and create an app.
  2. Set Authorization Callback Domain to localhost.
  3. Copy its Client ID and Client Secret in below.
Your phone
Used to sync photo geolocations, workouts and your location in the background.

Everything else here is a file you export or an address this server can go and read. A phone is neither: it moves, it sleeps, and it decides for itself when to speak. So it is set up on the phone — Hexplore for iOS, Settings tab — and this page is where you see whether it is working.

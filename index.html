Import locations
KML, GPX, GeoJSON, CSV, or a JSON export from Google Timeline, Snapchat or Apple Photos.

Files are parsed in your browser — nothing leaves this device until you press Import, and then only the cells. You see what was found before anything is saved, and re-importing refreshes dates and counts instead of duplicating cells. Files that drew a line can keep it as a saved route as well. Fixes less than an hour apart in the same cell count as one visit, so a recorded activity doesn't drown out everywhere else you've been.

Strava
Your rides and runs, pulled in on a schedule.

Strava only talks to registered apps, so you make one of your own — it takes a minute and stays private to you.

  1. Open strava.com/settings/api and create an app.
  2. Set Authorization Callback Domain to localhost.
  3. Copy its Client ID and Client Secret in below.
